Make "Export Rune Build" work and add the missing champion-detail panels

Auditing the champion detail against the reference's own key list
(lol.common.champion.*) showed three gaps.

- "Export Rune Build" was a static heading. In the reference it is a button
  that writes the recommended rune page into the League Client and confirms
  it with a check icon and the `lol.common.rune-set` toast. It now calls the
  existing `lcu:apply-runes` handler with the page's perk ids.
- The Summoner Spells panel was missing. The main process now resolves the
  recommended spell ids against the summoner spell catalogue (cached six
  hours) and returns their names and icons, and the page renders them with
  their pick rate.
- The Support Item section was missing. It lists the situational items that
  follow the support quest item, with the quest ids filtered out, exactly as
  the reference does.

tools/verify-detail-panels.mjs opens the modal over CDP and lists the panels
it rendered.
