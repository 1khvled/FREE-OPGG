Restore the letter grades on the ARAM / Mayhem overlay cards

The v2 overlay showed the numeric OP.GG tier (OP / Tier 1 .. Tier 5) on the
champion badge, the ARAM bench cards and the augment cards. The overlay has
always shown a letter grade there, and the numeric tier is the wrong headline
for a card you read at a glance in-game.

- overlay/parts/normalize.js: a tier now carries a letter grade
  (S+ for the OP tier, then S, A, B, C, D) alongside the numeric label, and
  understands both the numeric tiers and the `Tier N` / `OP` labels the main
  process returns.
- overlay/parts/section-champion.js, section-bench.js, section-augments.js:
  the badge shows the letter; the numeric tier stays in the tooltip.
- overlay/overlay.css: the letter-grade swatches with the S+ gold gradient
  and the S blue glow, and the S+ / S / A / B card treatments.

tools/test-overlay-tier.mjs asserts the mapping (21 cases).
tools/verify-overlay.mjs injects a mock ARAM/Mayhem state into the overlay
store and captures the HUD, so the cards can be checked without a League
Client.
